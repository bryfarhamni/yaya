# Research Article Writer

This skill applies the rules in `references/format-penulisan.md`. **Read that file before writing or reviewing**: it is the source of truth, and its examples show what is and is not acceptable. The checklist below is a summary for quick checking.

Write in the same language the user writes in or asks for (usually Bahasa Indonesia). Keep foreign terms and Latin species names in *italics* (e.g. *single dose*, *A. lumbricoides*).

## Article structure

Judul → Nama dan institusi penulis → Abstrak dan kata kunci → Pendahuluan → Metode → Hasil → Pembahasan → Kesimpulan → Ucapan terima kasih → Daftar pustaka.

## Checklist per section

**Judul**
- Title case; not underlined, not in quotation marks, no full stop at the end.
- About 12 words, not counting words like *dan, dari, pada, dalam*. Neither too long nor too short.
- Stated as a statement, not a question (questions only for opinion or editorial pieces). It should not state the conclusion.
- No abbreviations except widely known ones. Lowercase *the* inside the title.
- Match it to the research problem and aim. Add place and time only when the findings are bound to them (e.g. epidemiology). Including them means the findings can't be generalised.

**Penulis**
- Names without academic titles, written the same way each time; abbreviate only the middle name (Muhamad A. Fathoni, not M.A. Fathoni / Muhamad AF).
- Superscript numbers link each author to an institution. Authorship can be justified with the five-category contribution score (100 points).

**Abstrak**
- One paragraph or structured (Pendahuluan / Metode / Hasil / Simpulan), 150–200 words.
- Covers: why the study was done, what was done, what was found, what it means.
- No citations. A sentence may start with a numeral (only allowed in the abstract).
- 4–8 keywords, mostly nouns, ordered from general to specific.

**Pendahuluan**
- 1–2 paragraphs, at most one page: background and earlier work → problem → aim.
- Tujuan umum = the study's output. Tujuan khusus = specific, measurable items that answer the research questions.

**Metode**
- Narrative sentences, not instructions. Covers design, place and time, population and sample (inclusion/exclusion criteria, sampling, sample size), procedure and measurement, ethics approval, and data analysis (tests, significance level, software).

**Hasil**
- Report data without comment, in the order of the tujuan khusus.
- Text, tables, or figures, but never repeat table data in the text. Write tight sentences with no repetition (see the "Kurang baik / Lebih baik" examples in the reference).
- Tables: title-case caption, a heading on every column, no vertical lines, about one table per 1,000 words, referenced in the text (Tabel 1). Don't make a table for data that fits in one sentence, or for a list that isn't data. Don't repeat p or CI in the text when they're in the table.

**Bilangan, statistik, persentase**
- A number that fits in one word is written in words, unless it's part of a series. A number at the start of a sentence is written in words, or the sentence is rephrased / begins with *Sebanyak*.
- A single digit followed by a metric unit uses numerals (8 mg). Numbers of two or more digits use numerals.
- Mean: one decimal place. SD/SE: one or two decimal places more than the raw data. t, χ², r: two decimal places.
- Write the exact p-value (p=0,023), not p<0,05. Use a decimal comma in Indonesian text.
- Percentages: one decimal place when n>100; percentages are acceptable when n>40; no decimals for moderate n; no percentages for very small n ("1 dari 2 pasien").

**Pembahasan**
- Answer the research problem. Explain what the findings and statistics mean, compare them with the literature (agree? disagree, and why? different method or population?), stress what is new, and state relevance, implications, and limitations.
- Don't restate the results. Don't open sentences with the same phrase over and over ("Pada penelitian ini…", "Hasil penelitian menunjukkan…").

**Simpulan**
- Answers each tujuan khusus in a short paragraph of prose.
- Firm wording: no *mungkin, barangkali, tampaknya, cenderung, agaknya*.
- Nothing that goes beyond the aims (no policy recommendations that are not tested by the study).

## Modes

- **Drafting**: ask for whatever you need and don't have (design, setting, n, key results, aims), then write each section following the checklist. Never invent data, statistics, or citations. Mark any gaps as `[…]`.
- **Reviewing**: quote the offending text, name the rule it breaks (section + rule), and give a corrected version. Group findings by section in article order, and end with a short list of the most important fixes.
