# AI writing patterns to remove

Lecturers notice these. Each item lists what to look for and what to do instead. English first; Bahasa Indonesia equivalents are included for Indonesian-language decks. Flag a pattern only when it actually appears; one occurrence of a common word is not a problem, repetition is.

## Words and phrases

| Pattern | Examples (EN / ID) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Inflated verbs and adjectives | delve, crucial, pivotal, vital, robust, comprehensive, intricate, multifaceted, landscape, realm, tapestry / *sangat krusial, sangat penting, komprehensif, holistik, signifikan* (when not statistical) | Plain word, or cut |
| Empty openers | "In today's world…", "It is important to note that…", "In conclusion, it is clear that…" / *Di era modern ini…, Perlu diketahui bahwa…, Tidak dapat dipungkiri bahwa…, Seiring dengan perkembangan zaman…* | Start with the point |
| Hedge stacking | "may potentially", "could possibly help to" / *dapat berpotensi, mungkin bisa* | One hedge, or none if the source is firm |
| Overstatement | "revolutionary", "game-changer", "plays a key role in every aspect" / *memegang peranan yang sangat vital dalam segala aspek* | State the measured effect with its number and source |
| "Not only… but also" and "It's not X, it's Y" framing | / *Bukan hanya… tetapi juga…* used repeatedly | Say the claim directly |
| Signposting the obvious | "Let's explore…", "This slide will discuss…" / *Mari kita bahas…, Pada slide ini akan dijelaskan…* | Cut; the title already says it |

## Structure

- **Rule of three everywhere**: every list has exactly three items, every sentence has three adjectives. Vary it.
- **Uniform rhythm**: every bullet the same length and grammatical shape; every note paragraph the same length.
- **Mirror closers**: each slide note ends with a one-line moral or summary ("This shows how important X is"). Use a real transition instead.
- **Em-dash chains and colons** used for drama.
- **Generic conclusion** that could fit any topic ("Together we can build a healthier future"). Replace with the actual findings.
- **Bold-everything**: bolded phrases in most bullets.

## Content signals (these are also hallucination risks)

- Round, unsourced numbers ("about 50% of people", *sekitar 70%*).
- Vague attribution: "studies show", "experts agree", "research has proven" / *penelitian menunjukkan, para ahli sepakat* with no citation.
- A citation that looks right but whose title, year, journal, or DOI doesn't match a real paper.
- A claim attributed to a source that the source doesn't make, or makes more weakly.
