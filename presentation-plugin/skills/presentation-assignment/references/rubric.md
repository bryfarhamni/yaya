# Default rubric: literature review presentation

Used when the brief has no rubric. 100 points. Edit the weights and descriptors to match your course.

| # | Criterion | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Content accuracy | 25 | Every fact is correct and matches its source; no invented or overstated claims; numbers carry citations |
| 2 | Coverage and depth | 15 | Covers what the assignment asks for (definition, epidemiology, etiology, pathophysiology, diagnosis, management or prevention as relevant), explained beyond textbook bullet level |
| 3 | Synthesis | 15 | Compares sources: where they agree, disagree, or have been updated; names gaps; not a source-by-source summary |
| 4 | Sources and citation | 15 | Standard current textbook editions plus recent supporting literature; Vancouver superscript citations on the slide where used; complete, correctly formatted reference list |
| 5 | Structure and flow | 10 | Introduction explains why the problem happens and lays the basic concepts; body answers each question in a logical order, general to specific; conclusion gives short key takeaways |
| 6 | Slide design | 10 | Visual hook opening; one idea per slide, ≤5 short bullets; text 24pt+ with high contrast; numbers shown as charts, not tables; book figures where they explain better than text |
| 7 | Delivery and timing | 5 | Fits the time limit (±1 minute); speaker explains rather than reads; involves the audience at least once |
| 8 | Q&A | 5 | Answers likely questions accurately with sources, using backup slides where needed |

Grade bands per criterion: full marks / about two thirds (minor gaps) / about one third (major gaps) / zero (missing).
